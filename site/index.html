01

A thread back to the source

  1. Approve the sources

    The plan starts with project-scoped access, not an indiscriminate repository dump.

  2. Keep the distinctions

    Evidence, your decisions, and uncertain interpretations stay separate, with provenance and known gaps.

  3. Review the handoff

    A bounded execution brief would carry intent, constraints, relevant context, and proposed checks into your host.

02

Your coding host stays your workspace

Existing hosts retain chat, tools, execution, and permissions. Threadmark is designed to prepare context—not become another executor.

The planned shell is an occasional tray, management, and review surface. Not a chat app, editor, dashboard, or agent launcher.

03

Design direction, not shipped capability

Local knowledge
A Rust core with the provisional mem-z knowledge layer: SQLite-backed evidence, retrieval, and editable decisions.
Replaceable extraction
Graphify is an initial candidate worker, not the authority for knowledge semantics. Inferred relationships would remain labeled.
Explicit inference
Structural analysis is planned without a model. Semantic work requires explicitly authorized inference, content scope, and budgets—not reused coding-host credentials.
Open questions
Storage, background orchestration, inference, shell behavior, and host compatibility still need implementation and runtime validation. No performance or completeness promises.
